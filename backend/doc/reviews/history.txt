================================================================
  REVIEW HISTORY — GET /v1/reviews/history
  Base URL: http://localhost:3001
  Auth: Reviewer ONLY (permission: review_projects)
  Rate limit: 60 requests per minute
================================================================

The reviewer's OWN past reviews (paginated), newest first, including
the project info and current average/count on that project.

Query params: page, limit (same as available).
